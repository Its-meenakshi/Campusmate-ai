# 🎓 CampusMate AI

## AI-Powered Virtual Student Assistant

CampusMate AI is an AI-powered student assistant chatbot designed to support
college students with academic, career, internship, skill-development, and
general student guidance.

The project addresses **SDG 4 – Quality Education** by providing accessible
and personalized guidance through a conversational AI system.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

To make academic and career guidance more accessible, personalized, and
convenient for college students through an AI-powered virtual student assistant.

---

## 🌍 Sustainable Development Goal

### SDG 4 – Quality Education

CampusMate AI supports SDG 4 by helping students access personalized
academic and career guidance through an AI-powered conversational system.

---

## 🤖 Key Features

- 🎓 Academic-year based personalization
- 📚 Academic guidance
- 📝 Exam preparation guidance
- 💼 Career preparation
- 🛠️ Skill recommendations
- 🚀 Internship guidance
- 📄 Resume and interview guidance
- 💬 Conversational AI assistance

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. The student starts a conversation with CampusMate AI.
2. The chatbot asks which year the student is currently studying in.
3. The student selects 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year.
4. The selected academic year is remembered during the conversation.
5. The student asks a question related to academics, careers, internships,
   skills, or general student guidance.
6. The AI provides a relevant response based on the student's context.
